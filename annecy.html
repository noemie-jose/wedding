Annecy

Make a Weekend of It

Annecy sits at the top of a turquoise lake with the Alps behind it, an old town threaded with canals, and Mont Blanc about an hour away. If you're flying this far, stay a few extra days.

The Old Town

Canals, pastel houses and the Palais de l'Isle, the little 12th-century prison sitting mid-canal that every photo of Annecy is of. Markets fill the streets on Tuesday, Friday and Sunday mornings.

The Lake

Among the cleanest in Europe and still swimmable in September. Rent a small electric boat or a paddleboard, or take the ferry down to Talloires and back.

The Cycle Path

A flat, car-free path runs most of the way around the lake's western shore. Rent bikes in town and ride to Duingt for lunch, about 15 km each way, with places to swim along the route.

Up High

Drive or hike up the Semnoz for the whole lake in one view, or walk to the Château de Menthon. The Col de la Forclaz is where the paragliders launch, and you can take a tandem flight down to the water.

Gorges du Fier

A walkway bolted into a narrow river canyon 20 minutes out of town. Short, strange and worth it.

Day Trips

Chamonix and Mont Blanc are about 1h15 by car. Geneva's old town is 45 minutes. Lyon, for a food weekend, is under two hours.

Eat

This is Savoie: tartiflette, raclette, fondue, lake fish, and Reblochon on everything. The mountain food is heartier than the September sun suggests, so pace yourself before Saturday.

Getting Around

The town itself is walkable. For lake villages and trailheads, a rental car or the regional buses do the job.

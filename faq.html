Questions

The Practical Bits

If something isn't here, ask us and we'll add it.

Wait, aren't you already married?

Yes. We were married on 18 June 2026 at Cambridge City Hall, with our parents, grandparents and siblings there. It was small and lovely and about ten people deep.

This is the celebration with everyone else: our wider community, in one place, for one weekend. There will still be a ceremony on the Saturday: vows, tears, the lot.

When and where exactly?

Saturday 18 September 2027, in Annecy, in the French Alps. The welcome drinks are on Friday the 17th and there's a farewell brunch on Sunday the 19th. The venue and times are coming, so check The Weekend.

How do I get there?

Fly into Geneva (GVA). Annecy is about 45 minutes south by car, or an hour and a quarter on a direct coach from the airport. Full details, including typical flights from Boston, New York, Portland, Brussels, Amsterdam and Barcelona, are on the Travel & Stay page.

Where should I stay?

We're holding a block of rooms for the weekend and will post the booking link here as soon as it's confirmed. We'll email everyone who has RSVP'd when it opens. If you'd rather sort your own, staying in the old town or on the lake both work well.

When do you need my RSVP?

By 31 December 2026, please. It gives us the new year to get numbers to the venue. Earlier is genuinely helpful if you're flying a long way.

What should I wear?

We'll confirm the dress code closer to the time. Plan for an outdoor stretch, grass or gravel underfoot, and an evening that cools off, so bring a layer.

Can I bring someone?

Your invitation says who it's for, and the RSVP form asks for everyone's names. If you're not sure, just ask us.

Are children welcome?

Yes. Add them to your RSVP with their ages so we can plan food and seating.

What about gifts?

There's no registry and we're not asking for gifts. You're travelling to another country to be with us. That's more than enough.

Do I need a visa?

Most visitors don't for a short stay, but the EU's ETIAS authorisation is expected to be required by 2027. It's an online form and a small fee. Check the official guidance a few months out, and make sure your passport has at least six months left on it.

Will there be transport to the venue?

Possibly a shuttle from the hotels, depending on where everyone ends up staying. We'll confirm on The Weekend page.

I have a dietary restriction.

Tell us on the RSVP form. There's a box for allergies and preferences, and the caterer will get the list.

Who do I ask about anything else?

Just write to us directly. We'd rather answer twice than have you guess.
